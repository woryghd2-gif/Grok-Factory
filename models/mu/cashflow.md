# Micron Technology, Inc. (`mu`) — cash flow

As of 2026-10-25. Units: US$ millions. A = reported. E = model. Cash is cash, cash equivalents, and restricted cash, the cash-flow-statement total. Ending cash equals that balance-sheet sum in every year. Generated by `models/mu/build_model.py`.

Forecast operating cash flow starts at net income and adds depreciation and stock-based compensation. It subtracts finance-lease interest, which is capitalized into PP&E and therefore is not in net income, and it subtracts the increases in receivables and inventory and adds the increases in payables and other current liabilities. There is no unidentified 'other' line in the forecast. Customer-deposit repayment is financing, matching the FY2026A classification of deposit proceeds. The operating lines sum to operating cash flow. Operating cash flow plus investing, financing, and the exchange-rate line equal the net change, and beginning cash plus that change is ending cash. Gross capex and free cash flow sit below the roll so they are not a second cash outflow.

Net capex: FY2027E $55,000 = guided first-half $25,000 + a model second half of $30,000. The remarks said the second half is higher and that construction grows faster than equipment, and they did not give the year. $30,000 is $5,000 above the first half, not a doubling. FY2028E $46,000 and FY2029E $40,000 step down from that construction year and stay above FY2026A net capex of $27,367 because Idaho 2, New York, Hiroshima, and Singapore are still being built. Gross cash spend in the working-capital forecast equals net capex × (30,712 / 27,367), the FY2026A ratio of gross spend to net capex. Book PP&E increases by net capex plus capitalized lease interest and decreases by depreciation. Depreciation = 16% of opening net PP&E + 8% of the year's net capex (half-year convention). On FY2026A that rule returns $9,643 against reported depreciation of $9,503.

Historical investing cash flow is the 10-K total, which includes securities purchases and government-incentive proceeds as well as gross PP&E spend. Forecast investing cash flow is net capex only: marketable securities are held flat, and incentive proceeds are already inside the company's net-capex definition. Historical 'other operating' is other noncurrent liabilities plus the 10-K other line, so the operating block still sums to reported operating cash flow. The forecast has no residual. Free cash flow is operating cash flow minus net capex, which is the company's adjusted free-cash-flow definition. FY2025A $3,721 and FY2026A $62,308 reproduce the release. FY2024A company net capex was not obtained.
