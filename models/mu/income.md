# Micron Technology, Inc. (`mu`) — income statement

As of 2026-10-25. Units: US$ millions except per share, share counts (millions), and percents. A = reported. E = model. Depreciation is inside cost of goods sold and is also shown as a memo, so EBITDA = EBIT + depreciation. Generated by `models/mu/build_model.py`.

Forecast revenue and gross profit are the sum of the five segments in `segments.md`, minus unallocated cost of goods sold. Unallocated cost of goods sold in the forecast is the stock-based-compensation portion that Note 14 records in cost of goods sold (528 / 1,282 of total SBC). FY2027E R&D + SG&A + other operating expense equals FY2026A operating expenses plus the guided $2.5 billion, and the $500 patent-license charge does not repeat (other operating expense is set at $70). Eighty percent of the remaining R&D and SG&A increase is R&D. FY2028E and FY2029E add $1.0 billion of R&D a year (the announced Micron Research Labs plan is about $10 billion over a decade) and grow SG&A 8%. The tax rate is the guided 15.5% in each forecast year. Interest expense is the coupon on the notes that remain after the FY2026A prepayments ($109 million). Finance-lease interest is capitalized while construction is heavy, which is why FQ3 and FQ4 FY2026A printed no interest expense. Interest income is the 1-year Treasury par yield of 4.44% on the average of opening and ending cash, restricted cash, and marketable investments.

FY2027E gross margin has to clear the guided floor of about 85.95%. The script asserts that. The FY2029E ASP cut of 30% only moves gross margin from the high 80s to about 82%, because cash cost is a small share of the peak price. That is the explicit-year step. The mid-cycle reset to a 45% gross margin is the terminal year in `valuation.md`, not FY2029E. FY2026A other operating expense includes the $500 patent-license charge; FY2024A other operating income includes a $200 cross-license gain. Those are not repeated. Equity-method income is held at zero in the forecast because $59 million in FY2026A is not a driver and the investment asset is not modeled as a separate line. Other non-operating expense in FY2026A includes the $510 loss on debt prepayment, which is not repeated.
