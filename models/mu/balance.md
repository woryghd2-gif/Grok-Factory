Forecast policy, each one stated so none of them is a silent plug:

- Trade receivables = 60 days of annual revenue, next to the FQ4 FY2026A exit of 57.5 days. The FY2026A year-average of 88.6 days is the ramp, not the collection cycle. Other receivables (government incentives, taxes, other) stay at $4,404.
- Inventory = 142 days of annual cost of goods sold. That matches FQ4 FY2026A inventory against FQ4 cost of goods sold. The company's 129-day DIO is a different definition and is not used.
- Trade payables keep the FY2026A ratio to cost of goods sold. PPE payables = 28.1% of gross capex, the FY2026A ratio. Current income-tax payable = 25% of the year's tax provision. Compensation accruals scale with R&D + SG&A. The $396 of other accrued expenses stays flat.
- Other current liabilities = 8% of FQ4 revenue (the FY2026A price-protection accrual was $4,320 inside a $4,386 line, and 8% of FQ4 revenue reproduces it). FY2028E and FY2029E use one quarter of annual revenue as FQ4.
- Short-term and long-term marketable investments stay at the September 3, 2026 balances. Maturities are reinvested. Surplus cash stays in cash equivalents. No further customer deposits are forecast, because the timing of the undrawn commitment was not obtained. One-third of the $12,895 contract liability is repaid in FY2029E, the first of the three fiscal years inside the 2029–2031 window. The 10-K does not give a fiscal-year schedule; the one-third is the model timing.
- Goodwill, other noncurrent assets, unearned government incentives, and other noncurrent liabilities (including the $9.82 billion noncurrent tax payable) stay flat. Payment dates for that payable were not obtained, so it is not paid inside the forecast and it is subtracted in the valuation bridge instead.
- Debt: notes stay at carrying value $2,792. Finance leases amortize at the 4.71% effective rate against the undiscounted maturity schedule. No new borrowing and no discretionary note prepayment.
- Equity: retained earnings plus net income minus the dividend; additional capital plus stock-based compensation; treasury stock minus withholding at 45% of SBC. No open-market buyback. The $35.16 billion authorization is capacity from December 9, 2026, not a committed program, and the CHIPS award limits buybacks until that date. Diluted shares stay at the FQ1 guide of 1,150 million. Basic shares for the dividend stay at 1,131 million outstanding.
